How to read it

Beijing's spokespeople speak in set formulas. The same phrases recur across the Foreign Ministry, the Defense Ministry and the Taiwan Affairs Office, and analysts read changes in the mix as signals. This page is a reading aid for that mix. It is a heuristic, not a forecast. A formula's meaning depends on who says it, about whom, and what else happens that week.

Pick one of the sample statements or paste any text. The decoder highlights every formula in its dictionary, in Chinese or English, shaded by tier. Hover or tab to a highlight for a gloss; click it to see what the formula has signalled, how often TSM has recorded it, and dated examples that link to the official transcripts.

Tiers and temperature

TierWhat it means

The tiers are TSM's own ordering notional, not an official scale. The temperature is the highest tier found, plus 0.5 for each additional distinct formula at tier 3 or above, capped at +1.0 and at 5. Cool is up to 1.5, Mild up to 2.5, Warm up to 3.5, Hot up to 4.5, and Very hot above that. A statement with no dictionary formula shows no temperature; the dictionary does not cover every threat.

Method and sources

  • Dictionary. 43 formulations chosen and glossed by TSM. Glosses and "what it has signalled" notes are TSM's reading of how the phrases are used in the corpus; external facts carry their own source links in the panel.
  • Matching. Each formulation has a Chinese pattern and an English pattern (shown under "Matching rule"). English translations vary, so the English patterns cover common renderings, not every one. Matching runs entirely in your browser.
  • Counts and examples. TSM's PRC official-statement corpus: 1,565 Taiwan-related Q&A items from Foreign Ministry, Defense Ministry and Taiwan Affairs Office briefings and releases, 4 July 2022 to 30 September 2026 (736 MFA, 260 MND, 569 TAO). Taiwan Affairs Office releases that repeat an answer from the same day's press-conference transcript, and Defense Ministry answers held twice, are counted once. A statement counts when the English pattern matches its English answer or the Chinese pattern matches its Chinese answer. Chinese text is held mainly for 2026 items, and TSM widened collection in 2026 (every MFA Q&A, all three MND channels, TAO from April), so rates before and after 2026 are not strictly comparable. The 2026 H2 bar covers July to September only.
  • Translations. Examples are labeled by provenance: official English, TSM translation of the Chinese transcript, machine translation by Google (Taiwan Affairs Office, May and June 2026 and some of July to September), machine translation by Claude (Anthropic; most Taiwan Affairs Office items from July to September 2026, where Google refused requests), or Chinese only. Machine translations have not been checked by a person; the Chinese original is shown with them. Most Foreign Ministry examples are TSM translations of the Chinese transcript; the Ministry's own English on the linked page is worded differently. The two Foreign Ministry sample statements use the official English. Chinese-only items are Defense Ministry briefings for which the Ministry had published no official English by 30 September 2026 (16 and 25 June, 4 September, and the 20 May statement). The 24 September press conference now carries the official English. They can match only the Chinese patterns.
  • Transcript sources. fmprc.gov.cn, mfa.gov.cn, eng.mod.gov.cn, mod.gov.cn, gwytb.gov.cn. Most Defense Ministry items before May 2026 were stored without their URL and link to the Ministry's spokesperson page.
  • External references. UNGA Resolution 2758 (XXVI); Anti-Secession Law; Xi Jinping's July 2021 centenary speech; U.S. Office of the Historian on the Shanghai Communiqué and normalization; CSIS ChinaPower on Joint Sword-2024A.
