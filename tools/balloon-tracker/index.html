How to read this tracker

Taiwan's Ministry of National Defense (MND) publishes a report on PLA activity around Taiwan every morning. When its forces detect a PRC balloon, the report adds a balloon section. In December 2023, early January 2024 and December 2024 that section gave each balloon's detection time, altitude, heading and position, written as a distance and bearing from a Taiwanese city. From mid-January to April 2024 the English reports carry no balloon section, and in January 2025 several English report pages have no text at all; TSM's sheet fills some of those days with counts. From February 2025 MND gives a count only.

The map shows only balloons whose position MND stated. Pick a season at the top, then a dot, a bar on the timeline or a report in the list. The panel quotes MND's words and links to the report.

Every dot is approximate. TSM computes it by going the stated distance from the city center on the stated bearing. MND rounds distances to whole nautical miles and uses compass words such as "northwest", so a dot can be several kilometers from where the balloon was. Days without a stated position have no dot.

Method and sources

  • Balloon reports: Taiwan Ministry of National Defense, PLA Activities list (English). All reports from to were read; the balloon section of each is quoted in full on this page. The report pages are fetched directly (MND's certificate chain fails validation, so the build script skips TLS verification).
  • TSM sheet: the "balloon" column of TSM's PLAAF Activity Around Taiwan spreadsheet (TSM local). Where the sheet has a count and no MND report text mentions balloons, the day appears as "sheet only". Where both exist and disagree, the panel says so.
  • Reference points: coordinates from each place's Wikipedia article (city centers, and the air base's listed coordinates for Ching-Chuan-Kang): . MND does not say which point in each place it measures from.
  • Computation: great-circle destination from the city on the bearing (north = 0°, northwest = 315°, and so on) for the stated distance (1 nautical mile = 1.852 km). Dates are the start of MND's 6 a.m.-to-6 a.m. reporting window; times are as MND gives them.
  • Build: scripts/build.py regenerates data/balloons.js from the report pages and the sheet.
