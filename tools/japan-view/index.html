How to read it

Japan's Ministry of Defense Joint Staff Office (JSO) publishes a short release when its forces see Chinese navy ships pass through the straits and channels of Japan's island chain. Each release names the ships by class and hull number, says where they were first seen, and says which way they crossed. This tool turns every such release from January 2025 to September 2026 into one row per crossing.

On the map, each strait has two arrows, one for each direction. "Toward the Pacific" means ships leaving the East China Sea or the Sea of Japan; the opposite arrow means ships coming back. The number beside an arrow counts crossings in the window you picked. Click an arrow to open its latest crossing. Pick a ship in the panel and numbered circles trace every crossing Japan reported for that hull. Diamonds show carrier groups and a China-Russia joint sailing at the positions Japan gave, turned into map points from "about N km south of an island".

The timeline counts crossings per month, colored by strait. The dark line is Taiwan's Ministry of National Defense count of PLA Navy ships around Taiwan, averaged over each month's reporting days. Shaded bands mark PLA exercises around Taiwan; ticks under the axis mark joint combat readiness patrol days announced by MND. Click a month to show it. Shift-click a strait in the panel to show only that strait.

Sources and method

  • Crossings: Japan Ministry of Defense, Joint Staff Office press releases "中国海軍艦艇の動向について" (Movements of Chinese Navy vessels) and "中国及びロシア海軍艦艇の動向について", January 2025 to September 16, 2026. Each row links its PDF on mod.go.jp/js. The JSO press index blocks scripted access, so the list of releases came from Wayback Machine copies of September 28, 2026 and October 2, 2026 (no Chinese navy release after September 16); the PDFs themselves were downloaded from mod.go.jp. A probe of unlisted release numbers for every date found one more release, dated July 20, 2026, on the China-Russia group's July 19 position and gunnery drill. Its text is identical to the listed July 21 release (p20260721_01) apart from the date; the tool cites the July 20 copy.
  • Coding: a draft parser read the Japanese text; every row was then checked by hand, and a build script confirms each hull number and date appears in its release. One row is one crossing of one strait by one group of ships as the release describes it. Where a release says a ship is "the same one" that crossed earlier, the earlier crossing is not counted again. The date is the day the release gives for the crossing, or the first day of a range. Class names are JSO's NATO reporting names (Luyang III, Jiangkai II and so on).
  • Directions are JSO's (for example "南東進", heading southeast). "Toward the Pacific" and similar labels group them by the side of the strait the ships headed for.
  • Arrow and diamond positions are drawing aids. Arrows sit at a fixed point in each strait. Diamonds are computed from the release's "about N km [direction] of [island]" using approximate island centers, so they can be off by tens of kilometers. Points that fall off the map are listed in the panel.
  • Not counted: carrier operations away from the straits (shown as episodes), June 2025 dual-carrier movements that JSO gave only in release annexes, Russian ships, and PLA aircraft. Carriers Liaoning (16) and Shandong (17) are listed by name.
  • Taiwan MND counts: TSM daily data, Taiwan Ministry of National Defense reports, PLA Navy ships per 06:00 to 06:00 window, through October 1, 2026. Joint combat readiness patrol days are rebuilt from MND press releases. See TSM's Strait Snapshot.
  • Exercises: TSM "Exercises as Theater" verified event list: February 26, 2025 drill (RFA), Strait Thunder-2025A and Justice Mission-2025 (Global Taiwan Institute).
  • Coverage limits: the counts show what Japan detected and chose to publish. JSO released no crossings between January 28 and March 29, 2026, and only five between Yonaguni and Taiwan. PLA Navy ships that operate east of Taiwan without passing through Japan's straits do not appear, and no submarine crossings appear in these releases. Treat every number here as a floor, not a total.
  • Basemap: Natural Earth.
