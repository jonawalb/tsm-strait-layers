How to read this atlas

Every morning, Taiwan's Ministry of National Defense (MND) publishes a short report on PLA activity around Taiwan over the previous 24 hours. The report says how many aircraft were detected, how many crossed the Taiwan Strait median line or entered Taiwan's air defense identification zone (ADIZ), and which parts of the ADIZ they entered, for example "northern, central, southwestern and eastern ADIZ". It also attaches a map of flight paths.

This atlas reads the text of every report in MND's English list and counts, for each sector, the days on which MND said aircraft entered it. Pick a window on the timeline or press Play; the map, the sector bars and the list of days update. Click a sector to highlight its days on the timeline. Click a day to read MND's own words and follow the link to its report and flight-path map.

What the numbers are not. MND gives one count for all sectors together, so this page counts days per sector, not aircraft per sector. A day that names three sectors counts once in each. The sector shapes on the map are TSM's approximate drawing: MND names sectors but does not publish their boundaries.

Method and sources

  • Reports: Taiwan Ministry of National Defense, PLA Activities list (English), every report from to ( reports). Report text was fetched from each report page. TSM's archive of report texts (adiz_cache.json, Nov 2020 to Mar 2026) was the fallback where a page could not be fetched.
  • Parsing: scripts/build.py reads each report's text for the reporting window, the aircraft total, MND's median line/ADIZ figure and the sector words after "entered". "Southwest" and "southwestern" count as one sector, and so on. Rarely used names (western, northwestern, southern) are listed in the panel but not drawn. Flight-path images are not read or digitized.
  • Dates: from the report for November 15–16, 2022 each report covers 6 a.m. to 6 a.m. (UTC+8) and names the median line and ADIZ sectors; a day on this page is the window's start date, which matches TSM's daily data. From August 3 to mid-November 2022 MND listed PLA aircraft around Taiwan by calendar day and named no sectors, so those days carry a total but no sector. Earlier reports covered a single day and were issued only when aircraft entered the southwestern ADIZ, so a blank day before August 2022 means no report, not a confirmed zero.
  • JCRP days: the joint combat readiness patrol flags in TSM's daily dataset, rebuilt from MND press releases from December 2022. Days before that carry no flag.
  • ADIZ outline: TSM's Taiwan ADIZ layer. Sector regions: drawn by TSM for display, approximate, not official.
  • Median line / ADIZ figure: MND's wording changed over time ("crossed the median line or entered", "crossed the median line and entered", "entered"). The page sums the figure as MND gives it.
