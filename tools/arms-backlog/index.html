    How to read it

    Each row is one Foreign Military Sales case that the U.S. government has notified to Congress and that Taiwan has not yet fully received. The bar starts on the notification date and runs to the data date. Its length is how long Taiwan has been waiting.

    An orange stretch means deliveries have started but the case is not finished. A dashed tail runs to the completion date most recently reported by Taiwan's Ministry of National Defense, a contract notice or the press. Diamonds mark a contract award or signed Letter of Offer and Acceptance (LOA). Small ticks mark other milestones, such as the ministry listing a case as delayed. Hover or focus a mark to read it, and select a row for the full record and sources.

    Month by month, at the top, redraws Figure 1 and Table 1 for each monthly update, all in TSM's current figure style: the Cato Institute's November 2023 baseline and its January to December 2024 updates, then TSM's updates since January 2025. Each month's source line names its publisher. Use the arrows (or the ← and → keys) to step through the months. Select a wedge to list its cases, and a case for its record and its status in every update. Green rows were added that month, cases that left the backlog are struck through at the bottom, and numbered notes mark places where the published post differs from its own data.

    The second chart shows the backlog's dollar total. Monthly total is the figure published each month: the Cato Institute's updates from January to December 2024, then TSM's since January 2025. How it piled up stacks today's open cases by the date each was notified, so you can see which years produced the weapons Taiwan is still waiting for.

    Method

    The universe of cases is what the U.S. notified to Congress under the Arms Export Control Act, as tracked by the Taiwan Security Monitor. Maintenance, spare-parts and training cases are left out. A case keeps its full notified value until final delivery; TSM does not estimate partial values, so the headline overstates what is still owed where deliveries are under way. Notified values are ceilings, and contracts often come in lower.

    Milestone dates carry the precision of their source. A year-only date is drawn at mid-year, and a year-only completion at year end. Expected dates are what officials or contracts said at the time, not forecasts by TSM, and many have slipped before.

    Category subtotals. The Traditional, Asymmetric and Munitions subtotals in this tool are summed from the individual cases: Traditional $12,927 million, Asymmetric $14,463 million and Munitions $2,329 million in August 2026, for a total of $29,719 million. These match Table 1 of TSM's August 2026 update as published when last checked (Sept. 29, 2026).
