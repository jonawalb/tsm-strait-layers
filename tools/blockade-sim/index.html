    Evidence the model leans on

    Red Sea, 2023 to 2024

    Daily transits, weekly mean (IMF PortWatch). Owners left a listed area within weeks while most ships were never attacked. The model's speed of avoidance is fitted to this drop.

      Past PLA drills around Taiwan

      Mean daily Taiwan Strait transits and Taiwan port calls: 28 days before vs during each exercise (IMF PortWatch). None thinned traffic, which fits the view that coercion so far has stopped at the precedent-setting stage.
