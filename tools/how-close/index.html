      The numbers

      For scale

      Shaded bands are the standard ballistic-missile range classes (short, medium, intermediate, intercontinental), shown only as a yardstick for distance. All of Taiwan's main island lies within 1,000 km of the Fujian coast, the short-range band. This tool measures distance; it says nothing about what any force aims at.

      How to read it

      Pick a city and the map draws the shortest path over the Earth's surface, the great circle, from that city to Taipei. The solid orange line goes to Taipei; the thin dashed line goes to Pingtan, the part of Fujian Province closest to Taiwan. On a flat map a great circle often looks curved. It is still the shortest route.

      The card gives three distances: to Taipei, to the nearest point of the People's Republic of China, and the width of the Taiwan Strait for comparison. The comparison pair is two real cities from the same city list that sit about 126 km apart, chosen as close to your city as the list allows.

      The link in your address bar keeps your city, so you can send the exact view to someone else. Download card saves a 1200 × 630 image for posts.

      Why the distance matters

      The Taiwan Strait is narrow by the standards of oceans and wide by the standards of amphibious warfare. At about 126 km, it puts all of Taiwan's main island within the 600–900 km range that the CSIS Missile Defense Project gives for China's DF-15 short-range ballistic missile when launched from the Fujian coast. It is also wide enough that a ship making 10 knots needs about seven hours to cross open water. Both facts shape how Taiwan, China and the United States plan.

      Method and sources

      • Cities: Natural Earth, 1:50m populated places (public domain), 1,211 places with population and position. Map land and PRC/Taiwan outlines: Natural Earth 1:110m land and 1:50m admin-0 countries. The build script is in scripts/build_data.py.
      • Distances are great-circle distances on a sphere of radius 6,371 km, which is within about 0.5% of the true ellipsoidal distance. "Nearest PRC territory" measures to the Natural Earth 1:50m outline of the PRC, including Hong Kong and Macau, sampled about every 5 km, so it is approximate near the border.
      • Strait width: 126 km from Pingtan Island (Fujian) to the Hsinchu coast, measured on Natural Earth 1:10m land. This matches the narrowest width given by Wikipedia's Taiwan Strait article; the continental coast itself is about 135 km from Taiwan on the same data. The Pingtan point used for the dashed line is 119.78°E, 25.50°N.
      • Missile range classes: Arms Control Association, Worldwide Ballistic Missile Inventories: short-range under 1,000 km, medium-range 1,000–3,000 km, intermediate-range 3,000–5,500 km, intercontinental over 5,500 km.
      • Short-range missile range: CSIS Missile Defense Project, DF-15 (CSS-6), 600–900 km.
      • Travel times are illustrative. The airliner speed of 850 km/h and the ship speed of 14 knots are notional round numbers, applied to straight-line distance; real flights and voyages follow routes and take longer.
