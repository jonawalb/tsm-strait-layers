How to read it

Each day from 1996 through 2025 is tested at one open-water point in the Strait. A day is a go day when the largest waves and the strongest wind that day both stay under your limits, no fog is reported at the chosen shore station, and no tropical storm is inside the typhoon radius. A crossing and the landings behind it take more than a day, so a window opens on a day only when it and the next few days all pass.

The top grid shows every year. Pale columns mean bad weeks almost every year; a lone dark cell is one lucky year. The bar chart averages the thirty years. The selected week shows which test does the damage, how wide the spread of wind and waves is, which storms came close, and the moon, darkness and tide for that week in a year you pick.

The two months analysts cite, April and October, are shaded. Move the sliders and watch how their rank changes. October in particular depends on how much wave height you allow: in this record, wind and waves at the center point rise sharply from September to October.

Method

  • Wind: daily maximum 10 m wind from the ERA5 reanalysis (ECMWF), through the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API, at 25.0N 120.5E, 24.0N 119.5E and 23.0N 118.5E. Days run midnight to midnight, Taiwan time.
  • Waves: daily maximum significant wave height from ERA5's ocean-wave model (Open-Meteo Marine API, era5_ocean) at the same points. Significant wave height is the mean of the highest third of waves; single waves run higher. Reanalysis smooths peaks, so storm days read lower than a buoy would record.
  • Fog: the fog flag in NOAA's Global Surface Summary of the Day for Pingtan, Xiamen Gaoqi airport and Taichung airport. A flag means fog was reported at some point that day, on shore. Taichung has reports only for 1996 to 2000 and 2014 to 2025; days with no report pass the fog test. NOAA's files run to late August 2025.
  • Typhoons: IBTrACS v04r01, Western Pacific. A day fails when any 3- or 6-hourly position with winds of 34 kt or more (U.S., Japan Meteorological Agency or WMO estimate) lies within the radius of 24.0N 119.5E.
  • Tides: Taiwan's Central Weather Administration publishes tide predictions, but its open-data API needs a registered key, so this tool does not use it. Instead, 18 tidal constituents were fitted by least squares to hourly modelled sea level (Open-Meteo Marine API, January 2023 to September 2026) just off four ports. The fit misses by about 10 to 20 cm (root-mean-square; tested on 2026 data held out of the fit). No nodal corrections. Use it for spring and neap timing and rough range, not navigation.
  • Moon and sun: computed in your browser with the low-precision formulas of the original SunCalc library (after Meeus). Nautical dusk is when the sun is 12 degrees below the horizon.
  • Not modelled: surf on any beach, currents, rain, low cloud, visibility at sea, or anything military. Points are open water in mid-Strait, between the two coasts. The tool names no landing sites; tides are given only for public ports.

Sources

  • Ian Easton, The Chinese Invasion Threat: Taiwan's Defense and American Strategy in Asia (2017). Quoted as rating April and October the only two months of "good suitability" in 19FortyFive, January 2025. Summarized as March to May and September to October in War on the Rocks, June 2023. The tool shows both readings.
  • ERA5 wind: Open-Meteo Historical Weather API. ERA5 waves and modelled sea level: Open-Meteo Marine API. Hersbach et al., The ERA5 global reanalysis, Quarterly Journal of the Royal Meteorological Society (2020), Copernicus Climate Change Service.
  • Fog: NOAA NCEI Global Surface Summary of the Day, stations 58944 (Pingtan), 59134 (Xiamen Gaoqi), 46770 (Taichung).
  • Typhoons: NOAA NCEI IBTrACS v04r01, Western Pacific file; Knapp et al., The International Best Track Archive for Climate Stewardship (IBTrACS), Bulletin of the American Meteorological Society (2010).
  • Surf context: the U.S. Marine Corps set an interim surf limit of a 4-foot significant breaker height for its Amphibious Combat Vehicle after two mishaps in 2022 (CRS In Focus IF11755, updated May 2026). Surf is not modelled here.
  • Sun and moon formulas: SunCalc. Data build script: tools/crossing-windows/scripts/build.py.
