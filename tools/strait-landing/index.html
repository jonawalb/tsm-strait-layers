How to play

You plan the PLA's landing on Taiwan's west coast. Plan: pick a month, a main landing zone and when to go. Play: eight 12-hour turns from D-day to D+3. Each turn, choose how many amphibious ships and civilian ferries sail, what your strikes hit, and whether to storm a port, then press Resolve turn (or N). Win by holding a lodgment of at least 15,000 troops at 1.5 to 1 over the defenders, with a port or twice that many troops, when D+3 ends. You can switch sides and command Taiwan's defense instead.
