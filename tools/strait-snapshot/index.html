How to read this page

Each month, the Taiwan Security Monitor publishes a Strait Snapshot report built around six figures. This page redraws those six figures from TSM's data for any month of 2026. Pick a month at the top; every number and chart updates. Hover or tab into a chart and use the arrow keys to read daily values.

The headline tiles compare the month with the month before and with the same month a year earlier. When the two periods have different numbers of reported days, as with a partial month, the comparison uses the average per reported day and says so.

The charts show numbers, not interpretation. For analysis, read the published report linked at the top.

Sources and method

  • Daily activity: Taiwan Security Monitor PLA Activity Center, compiled from Taiwan Ministry of National Defense (MND) daily reports. Each MND report covers the 24 hours to 6 a.m. Taiwan time. Aircraft counts run from August 6, 2022 (one missing day, October 16, 2022). ADIZ entries run from August 7, 2022, left blank on days where TSM's figure could not be matched to MND's report. PLAN and official ships run from August 13, 2024. Rows from August 23, 2026 on are MND's own figures. Data through October 1, 2026.
  • Dating: a day on this page is the start of MND's 6 a.m.-to-6 a.m. reporting window, so each day's count comes from the report MND issues the following morning. TSM's September 2026 rows had been shifted one day late; the shared dataset now uses MND's values on the window-start date throughout.
  • 2024 and 2025 totals are summed from TSM's daily dataset. The published reports drew their 2024–2025 comparisons from a separate historical file, so their figures can differ from these. Months with missing days are marked; totals for those months undercount.
  • Joint combat readiness patrols and long-distance flights are the days TSM flags in its daily data, rebuilt from MND press releases from December 2022. On July 2, 2026 MND announced both on the same day.
  • CCG incursions: TSM CGA/CCG Incident Tracker 2026, compiled from Taiwan Coast Guard Administration releases. "Southwest of Taiwan" is the tracker's "Taiwan" category. Checked against CGA releases: the September 3 ships are CCG 1306 Putuoshan and 2502 Daishan (release), and four September incidents missing from the tracker version this page reads are added from CGA releases: September 7 Dongsha, September 8 afternoon, September 10 and September 11 Kinmen (all in Chinese).
  • Allied transits: TSM Taiwan Strait Transit Tracker. Between September 12, 2025 and January 16, 2026 it has a single entry (November 5, 2025), so that stretch is thinly covered.
  • Report archive: tsm.schar.gmu.edu/strait-snapshot. Reports by Ethan Connell and Jonathan Walberg (January to July 2026) and Ethan Connell and TSM Staff (August 2026). The published August report counts 171 aircraft; MND's daily reports for August sum to 173, which this page uses.
