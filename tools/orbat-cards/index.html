How to use it

Each card is one formation: a theater command, army, fleet, air force, missile base or garrison. The front gives the name, country and headquarters city. Flip it for its role, its main classes of equipment and the sources behind them. Show on map finds its headquarters city.

Compare puts two cards side by side with the distance between their headquarters and from each to Taipei. Quiz asks students to match units to regions, or a highlighted city to its unit, and keeps score.
