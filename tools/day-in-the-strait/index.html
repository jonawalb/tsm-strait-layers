Words on this page

PLA
The People's Liberation Army, China's armed forces. The PLA Navy (PLAN) is its naval branch.
ADIZ
Air defense identification zone: airspace beyond a country's sovereign territory in which it requires aircraft to identify themselves, in the Congressional Research Service's definition. Taiwan's defense ministry reports how many PLA aircraft crossed the Strait median line or entered Taiwan's zone.
Joint combat readiness patrol
The PLA's own name for activity in which its aircraft operate together with PLA Navy ships around Taiwan; Taiwan's defense ministry reports it as a "so-called" or "alleged" patrol. The ministry announces these separately, with the time the activity began.
Official ships
Chinese government ships other than navy warships, which the defense ministry counts separately.
China Coast Guard incursion
China Coast Guard ships, or occasionally other Chinese government ships, entering the restricted waters around Taiwan's outlying islands or operating near Taiwan, as reported by Taiwan's Coast Guard Administration.

How this story is built

Nothing on this page is written by hand for a particular day. Pick any date from January 1, 2026 to the last day in TSM's data (October 1, 2026), and the page assembles the story from three records: the Taiwan Security Monitor's daily count of PLA activity, its China Coast Guard incident tracker, and Taiwan's Ministry of National Defense announcements of joint combat readiness patrols.

Most of what is known about a day is a set of totals. Taiwan's defense ministry reports how many aircraft and ships it detected in a 24-hour window ending at 6 a.m., without saying when each one appeared. The page shows those as totals and does not spread them across the clock. Only events with a time in the source go on the clock.

Sources and method

  • Daily totals: Taiwan Security Monitor PLA Activity Center, compiled from Taiwan Ministry of National Defense daily reports; rows from August 23, 2026 on are MND's own figures. Each report states its window, for example "6 a.m. Sep. 15 (Tue.) to 6 a.m. Sep. 16 (Wed.) (UTC+8)": MND report of September 16, 2026; all MND daily reports.
  • Which window: TSM's row for a date holds MND's report for the 24 hours starting at 6 a.m. that day, issued the next morning. The page states the window for each day.
  • Joint combat readiness patrols: the day flag comes from TSM's daily data. Start times, sortie counts and release times come from MND press releases, linked from each clock entry (for example June 3, 2026). MND issued the January 23 and January 29, 2026 releases in Chinese only (January 23, January 29), and the September 30 release had no English version when it was checked on October 2 (September 30).
  • China Coast Guard: TSM CGA/CCG Incident Tracker (complete version of September 24, 2026), compiled from Taiwan Coast Guard Administration releases and checked row by row against them on the CCG Gray-Zone Map. Descriptions and hull numbers are as recorded, except that hulls the CGA did release are shown where the earlier public tracker said they were withheld. For three rows (January 14 Dongsha, January 19 and March 20 Kinmen) the clock uses the CGA release's times, because the tracker's timeline has a typo. Map circles show the tracker's location category, not a position.
  • Allied transits: TSM Taiwan Strait Transit Tracker (date, ship, country). Routes are not recorded; the arrow is schematic.
  • ADIZ definition: Congressional Research Service, China's Air Defense Identification Zone (ADIZ), R43894, 2015.
  • Basemap: Natural Earth. Taiwan ADIZ boundary: TSM GIS layer.
  • More: the CCG Gray-Zone Map and Strait Snapshot Live.
