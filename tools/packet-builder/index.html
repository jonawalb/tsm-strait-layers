How to use it

Pick a date range: the last seven days of TSM data, a calendar month, a listed PLA exercise (with a few days either side if you like), or any custom span up to a year. Then switch sections on or off. The preview on the left is the packet itself, laid out on US Letter pages exactly as it will print.

Download PDF makes a file in one click. Print or save as PDF opens your browser's print dialog, where "Save as PDF" gives a sharper file with selectable text. The page settings (Letter, no margins) are set by the packet, so the defaults work.

The address bar keeps every setting. Copy the link to share a packet, or to rebuild the same one later with newer data.

Method notes

  • Every figure is computed in your browser from the data modules that power the other TSM interactives. The packet adds no data of its own and contains no notional values.
  • Daily counts are Taiwan Ministry of National Defense reports as compiled and checked by TSM. Each day is MND's 06:00 to 06:00 reporting window, filed under its start date. Daily data start Aug. 6, 2022 (ships Aug. 13, 2024).
  • The comparison period is the same number of days ending the day before the range starts. Changes compare daily averages over days with data.
  • Statements are chosen by a fixed, published rule (theme matches, balanced across the three PRC bodies). Quotes are verbatim excerpts of the English text, with the translation source labeled.
  • The AIS section appears only for ranges that overlap TSM's live AIS window, Sept. 4–28, 2026.
  • Known gaps are printed in the packet where they apply: the transit tracker's thin coverage from September 2025 to January 2026 (one entry, November 5, 2025), the CCG tracker's June 2024 start, and days without TSM data.

Data sources

  • Daily PLA activity, patrol and long-range flight flags, allied transits: shared/data/tsm.js, built from TSM trackers and Taiwan MND daily reports.
  • China Coast Guard incidents: CCG Gray-Zone Map data, TSM's CGA/CCG Incident Tracker compiled from Coast Guard Administration releases.
  • Exercises: Anatomy of an Exercise data, from TSM's Exercises as Theater event list.
  • PRC statements: PRC Rhetoric Heatmap corpus of Foreign Ministry, Defense Ministry and Taiwan Affairs Office answers.
  • AIS: Dark Fleet Viewer data, TSM's AIS database fed by AISStream.io.
  • PDF export: html2canvas 1.4.1 and jsPDF 2.5.1, loaded from cdnjs only when you download.

Each packet ends with a sources page listing every link behind the sections you chose.
