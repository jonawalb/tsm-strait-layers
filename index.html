About this site

Why a password?

TSM Interactive is a working research site. Its data files are encrypted, and your browser decrypts them after you enter the access password. The password never leaves your browser. For access, contact the Taiwan Security Monitor at tsm.schar.gmu.edu.

Data policy

Counts and events come from TSM's trackers and the official sources they cite, such as Taiwan's Ministry of National Defense and Coast Guard Administration. Every tool lists its sources. Where a model needs a value nobody publishes, the tool marks it notional. Nothing is invented, and nothing is targeting-grade.
